About PaaniLink

A booking and dispatch system for water tankers, built for the City Water Board.

The problem we solve

In many neighbourhoods piped water arrives only a few hours a week, so residents depend on tankers filled at authorised hydrants. Bookings by phone lead to tankers promised twice, hydrants running past their daily quota and subsidised water sold at commercial rates.

PaaniLink puts every step online so a tanker, a driver and hydrant water are never promised twice, subsidy is not misused and every delivery can be traced.

What the system guarantees

  • No double booking of tankers, drivers or hydrant water
  • Daily hydrant quota respected
  • Subsidy limited per household each month
  • Every delivery traceable from booking to confirmation

Who uses PaaniLink

🏠

Customers

Book, cancel, confirm or dispute deliveries and see their bills.

📋

Zone officers

Verify subsidy applications, approve subsidised bookings and handle disputes.

🏭

Hydrant operators

Assign tankers and drivers and record when a tanker is filled and leaves.

🚚

Drivers

See their own trips and mark them arrived, delivered or failed.

⚙️

Administrators

Manage zones, hydrants, tankers, rates, quotas and accounts, and see city reports.
