How it works

Every booking is checked, approved where needed, dispatched and confirmed.

Register

Sign up with your CNIC, phone and address. Your address decides your zone.

Book

Choose a size, date and time slot. Commercial bookings are approved automatically.

Approval and dispatch

Subsidised bookings are approved by a zone officer. The hydrant operator assigns a tanker and driver.

Deliver and confirm

The driver delivers. You confirm or dispute the delivery and pay the bill.

Tanker rates

Rates are set by the Water Board. Subsidised bookings cost much less.

Zones we serve

Each zone is served by one or more hydrants.

- Registered customers
- Bookings made
- Deliveries confirmed
- Active hydrants

Common questions

Who can get a subsidised tanker?

Registered low income households, hospitals and mosques. Apply from your dashboard and a zone officer will verify your proof details.

Is there a limit on subsidised bookings?

Yes. Each subsidised customer has a monthly limit. Your dashboard shows how much you have used.

What happens if the delivery fails?

The driver records a reason, such as house locked or road blocked, and the booking is marked failed so it can be followed up.

What if I am not happy with a delivery?

Raise a dispute from your bookings. A zone officer will review it and decide the outcome.
